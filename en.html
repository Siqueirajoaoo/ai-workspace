Frequently asked questions

See all questions
What is this platform?

A workspace with documents, spreadsheets, tasks, projects, calendar, files and meetings, where an AI assistant uses these tools for you and shows every step.

Is the AI working yet?

Not yet. This version is building the platform's foundation. The assistant arrives in stage 3, first with a local command interpreter, without pretending to be a language model.

Where is my data stored?

Only in the browser you're using. Nothing is sent to servers. If you clear your browser data, the information is deleted.

Can the AI delete things without asking me?

No. Permanent deletions, messages to third parties, payments and permission changes always ask for your confirmation. And you can stop any run.

Can I use it with my company?

Companies, members and permissions arrive in stage 2. In this local version they are a demonstration: secure isolation between different people requires a server.
