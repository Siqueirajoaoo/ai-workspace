About the project

We're building a workspace where artificial intelligence is the center of the experience: an assistant that understands requests, uses the platform's tools and shows what it did.

The name is still provisional. The visual identity was designed so the brand can change without rebuilding the product.

Development happened in nine stages. We'd rather ship complete modules than many modules that only look finished. Where something depends on a server, the screen says so plainly.
